Help & Guide

What each part of Account Book does and how to use it.
Quick start
  1. Go to and create a few categories, each with a monthly limit (for example Food, Transport, Bills).
  2. Log every expense or income in . The purple + button, or the N key on a keyboard, takes you there from anywhere.
  3. Open to see this month at a glance and what is due soon.

Add Record

Log one expense or one income.

  • Pick a category, choose Expense or Revenue, type the amount, add a short note and the date, then tap Record.
  • Enter something often? Tap the star button to save it as a Quick Template, then add it with one tap next time.
  • Tap the camera button to scan a receipt: the amount is read from the photo and filled in for you.
  • The app warns you if you seem to be adding the same record twice.

Record List

Everything you have recorded, newest first.

  • Type in the search box to find records by note, category or tag.
  • Open Advanced filters to narrow by date range, expense or revenue, category, amount or tag.
  • Tap Edit to change a record, Delete to remove it (you get a few seconds to undo), or the paperclip to see its receipt photos.
  • Export CSV downloads the records currently shown, for Excel or Google Sheets.

Budget

Create categories and give each one a spending limit.

  • Add a category (for example Food) with a budget for the month, the year, or with no time limit.
  • Each card shows what you spent, what came in and how much of the budget is left. It turns orange at 80% and red when you go over.
  • Tap a card to see every record in that category. Use the month picker to look at another month.
  • Leave the budget at 0 if you only want to group records without a limit.

Statistics

Totals and a category breakdown for any week, month or year.

  • Choose Weekly, Monthly or Yearly, pick the date, and read the chart and the table below it.
  • The trend line compares this period with the previous one.
  • For the current month you also get a projection: your daily average and where the month is heading.

Insights

Short, automatic observations about your spending.

  • Open the view and read the list. Tap Refresh after adding new records.
  • Insights compare this month with last month, point out categories close to their budget, and spot weekend versus weekday habits.

Dashboard

Your month at a glance, on one screen.

  • The top cards show this month's expense, revenue and the difference.
  • Upcoming lists what is due in the next 14 days: subscription renewals, debts, and recurring bills or income. Red means overdue. Tap a row to open it.
  • Salary Allocation compares this month's income with the total of your monthly budgets.
  • Further down: this month versus last month, a cashflow chart, a spending heatmap (tap a day for details) and category trends.

Savings Goals

Save toward something specific, like an emergency fund or a trip.

  • Add a goal with a target amount and, if you like, a deadline.
  • Tap Add money on a goal each time you put money aside. Choose Deposit to add, Withdraw to take money out.
  • The bar shows your progress. With a deadline, the goal also tells you how much to save per month.
  • History lists every deposit and withdrawal. Deposits do not change your records or budgets.

Target

A checklist of things you want to achieve by a certain age.

  • Enter your current age, then add each target with the age you want to reach it by.
  • Targets are laid out as a timeline. Tick one off when you achieve it, or edit it in place.

Recurring

Bills or income that repeat every month, such as rent or salary.

  • Add a rule with the category, amount, a note and the day of the month it happens.
  • On that day the record is added for you automatically when you open the app. You can also tap Run Due Recurring.
  • Upcoming rules also appear on the Dashboard.

Debts

Money you lent to someone, or money you owe.

  • Add the person, choose Owes me or I owe, the amount, and a due date if there is one.
  • The summary shows how much is owed to you, how much you owe, and the difference.
  • Tap Mark paid once it is settled. Overdue debts show in red here and on the Dashboard.

Subscriptions

Services you pay for regularly, such as Netflix or a gym.

  • Add the service with its amount, whether it bills monthly or yearly, and the next renewal date.
  • The summary shows what all subscriptions cost per month and per year.
  • When a renewal is due, tap Pay & Record: an expense is logged and the renewal date moves forward one cycle.
  • Cancel a subscription you stopped so it no longer counts.

Settings

Preferences, alerts, templates and your data.

  • Set the currency symbol shown in front of amounts.
  • Turn on Budget Alerts to get a notification at 80% of a budget and when you go over.
  • Manage your Quick Templates.
  • Export JSON saves a full backup; Import JSON restores one. Generate PDF makes a monthly report.

Handy things

  • Quick add: the purple + button (or the N key) jumps to Add Record. Press / to search records.
  • Assistant: the microphone button opens a chat. Type or speak, for example "spent 50 on fuel" or "今天吃鸡饭18块", to add records or ask what you spent.
  • Receipts: attach photos when adding a record; the paperclip on a record opens them.
  • Undo: after deleting a record, or adding money to a goal, an Undo button appears for a few seconds.
  • Offline: an Offline badge appears in the header when there is no connection. Keep using the app; changes sync when you are back online.
  • Dark mode: the moon or sun button in the header switches the theme.
  • Backups: your data is saved to your account in the cloud. Settings also lets you download a JSON backup.
